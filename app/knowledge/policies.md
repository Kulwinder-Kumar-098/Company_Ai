# Demo Policy Knowledge Base

Fictional sample policy text for local development only. Replace it with approved company documents.

## Annual Leave
Employees should submit annual leave requests through the internal HR portal. Requests should be submitted in advance and are subject to manager approval and applicable local policy. Contact HR for current balances and exceptions.

## Remote Work
Remote work arrangements require manager approval and must follow team security and availability requirements. Employees should protect confidential information and use company-approved devices and access methods.

## Expenses
Business expenses must be reasonable, documented with receipts, and submitted through the approved expense process. Reimbursement limits, deadlines, and exceptions must be confirmed in the official finance policy.

## Holidays
The company holiday calendar is maintained by HR. Employees should check the current calendar for office-specific dates and updates.
